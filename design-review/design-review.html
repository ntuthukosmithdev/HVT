Website design review

At a glance

A corporate website built to win the confidence of utilities, EPC contractors and industrial clients.

The new site presents HVT as an established Southern African engineering group: technically capable, safety-led and ready for major infrastructure work. It is a complete, working website, not a mock-up. Everything shown in this document is a screenshot of the working preview.

Groeipunt, Bokmakierie, Mount Edgecombe and Kaalfontein, each with its photograph gallery.

Company pages

About, Quality & Safety, Social Impact, Contact, Privacy Notice and Image Credits.

What the design sets out to communicate

  • Engineering excellence: precise, technically capable and dependable
  • Industrial credibility: real projects, shown in HVT’s own photographs
  • Safety and quality: SHEQ leadership and ISO 9001:2015 certification
  • Scale and ambition: work from 3.3kV to 765kV across the region
  • Southern African relevance: a long-term partner to Eskom and utilities

Built in from the start

  • Designed for phones and tablets as carefully as for desktop screens
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, with colour contrast chosen for readability
  • Search-engine foundations: page titles, descriptions, sitemap and company details
  • A project enquiry form with clear validation and POPIA-aware consent
  • No advertising or tracking cookies

Design direction

Design direction

Built on HVT’s existing identity, refined for a premium digital presence.

Colour

Signal amber#FFA033 → #F07800
Midnight navy#081321
Deep charcoal#171B20
Slate grey#647181
Light grey#F3F5F7

The amber is taken directly from the HVT logo’s gradient. It is used sparingly, for buttons, labels and key figures, so it signals importance. Navy, charcoal and soft greys carry the rest of the design.

Typography

Powering the infrastructure that moves Southern Africa forward.

Manrope for headings: confident and modern, with a two-tone treatment that gives headlines emphasis.

Inter for body text: highly legible at every size, on screens of every kind.

Logo

The supplied HVT artwork is used exactly as provided: the colour logo on light backgrounds, the reversed logo on dark, and the pylon symbol for the browser icon.

Photography

  • HVT’s own photographs for every project and service: real HVT sites and teams, captioned with the project they come from.
  • A few atmospheric images (dusk transmission lines and substations) in the hero and banners. These are openly licensed, labelled “Illustrative image” and never presented as HVT projects.
  • Dark, cinematic overlays give HVT’s daylight site photos a consistent, premium feel.

Before launch

What we need from HVT

Please confirm or update the following before the site goes live.

Credentials and figures

  • BBBEE Level 3 (BYO & BWO), CIDB 8EP / 7CE (PE) and ISO 9001:2015 are all current
  • Track-record figures still stand: 600+ reactive power projects (3.3kV–765kV), 95%+ of Eskom HV shunt capacitor banks, 8 crews accredited up to 765kV, supplier to Eskom’s 765kV build programme
  • Current status of ISO 14001 / ISO 45001, if HVT would like it shown
  • The CEO profile for Ganas (Boogie) Naidoo is up to date

Projects, people and permissions

  • Permission to quote Danie Bekker (Eskom) in the testimonial
  • Any project details HVT is happy to publish, such as scope, client, location and dates (Groeipunt, Bokmakierie, Mount Edgecombe)
  • Which social development initiatives are still current
  • Confirmation that HVT’s gallery photographs may be reused on the new site
  • The inbox that should receive website enquiries (currently info@hvt.co.za)
  • A review of the Privacy Notice by HVT’s compliance contact, and the name of HVT’s Information Officer
★

Recommended: a short professional photo shoot at an active HVT site at dusk or early morning. Replacing the illustrative hero images with HVT’s own cinematic photography would make the site stronger still.
