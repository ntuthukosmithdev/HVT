Add it to your email

Click Copy signature above first, then follow the steps for the app you use.

  1. Open Gmail in your browser and click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it “HVT”.
  3. Click in the signature box and paste with Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose “HVT” for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
  1. In the new Outlook or Outlook on the web, open Settings (gear icon), then Accounts, then Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, name it “HVT” and paste into the box with Ctrl + V.
  3. Set “HVT” as the default for new messages and replies/forwards.
  4. Click Save.
  1. In Outlook, go to File, then Options, then Mail, then Signatures….
  2. Click New, name it “HVT”, then paste into the editing box with Ctrl + V.
  3. On the right, choose “HVT” for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  4. Click OK, then OK again.
  1. Phone mail apps don’t keep logos or formatting, so click Copy mobile (plain text) version above. You can also type the lines in by hand.
  2. Gmail app: menu, then Settings, then your account, then Mobile signature. Paste and tap OK.
  3. iPhone Mail: Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature. Paste your text.
  4. Outlook app: Settings, then Signature. Paste your text.

The HVT logo in your signature loads from the HVT website. If a recipient’s email app blocks images until they allow them, they’ll see the text “HVT High Voltage Technology” in its place.
